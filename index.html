🎓Admission Information

Admissions Open: 2026-2027

Eligibility

  • Applicants must have passed Intermediate or an equivalent qualification.
  • Minimum 45% marks are required.
  • Students must meet the requirements of their selected program.

Required Documents

  • CNIC/B-Form copy
  • Matric certificate
  • Intermediate certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Domicile
  • Character certificate

Admission Procedure

  1. Collect or download the admission form.
  2. Complete the form carefully
  3. Attach the required documents.
  4. Submit the application before the deadline.
  5. Check the merit list.
  6. Complete the fee submission process
